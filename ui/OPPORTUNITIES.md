# Opportunities: rules (updated 6 Nov 2026)

One ranked list per CE, inside Analysis (Clusters | Opportunities), from the 5 Nov call with Aditya. Each section is
biggest first: clusters with a product before those without, then by searches or clicks. Every item is Agree or Set
aside. Cluster asks and Perf's call share one decision with the table's Perf ask column. Agreed Perf items go to Perf
with Review & send (its "From Opportunities" section and the package's `opportunities`); agreed growth items are listed
under "Stays with growth" and never go to Perf. Every decision is in the Changes log and can be undone.

| Type | Rule | Team |
|---|---|---|
| Cluster asks | Drafted by the opportunity tree on pages that sell (page type from the mapping prompt): bid, add to the main ad group, own ad group, land its ads on its page | Perf |
| Perf's call | Page informs rather than sells; searches at or above the CE minimum; coverage below the bar; Supply says a product serves it | Perf |
| Ads on the wrong page | Core ad group's final URL is not its own page; 100+ paid clicks; no landing ask covers it yet | Perf |
| Searches to add as keywords | Search term with enough clicks for about 10 conversions at the CE's conversion rate; ROI at or above target; not a close variant of a live keyword; by clicks | Perf |
| AI Max searches | Search term AI Max caught with no keyword of ours, above the same click threshold; by clicks | Perf |
| Searches no cluster takes | Paid search groups with no cluster, 50+ clicks: candidates for a new cluster | Growth |
| Revenue per click | Set-up complete, coverage still low: look at revenue per click against the cost-per-click trend | Growth |

## Hand check (2 Nov, old rules; kept for reference)

Each visible item checked against the table, the side panel and the source rows.

- **Keywords to add (10/10 plausible).** Polish and Dutch ticket searches, the audio-guide family, "vatican museums
  guided tour" and the Combo focus keyword are not bid. "Official site" keywords may be restricted by Google's
  policy on implying official status; Perf should judge those.
- **Clusters with no ad group (2/2).** Combo and Private Tours: their focus keywords are bid nowhere.
- **Searches to add as keywords (top 10: 10/10 factual).** Real searches with ROI 142–353%. Six of the ten are
  "official site" phrasing in other languages, so the same policy caveat applies.
- **Searches losing money (10/10 factual).** Mostly existing keywords at ROI 29–68%, e.g. "vatican tours rome" at 29%
  on $491. The fix is Perf's call (bid down, negative, or a better landing page).
- **Ad group lands on the wrong page (2/2).** Skip-the-Line and Vatican Gardens both have their own page; their core
  ad groups (generic Tickets) land on home.
- **Clusters that need a page (3/4).** Passes, Discount and Sistine Chapel Tickets & Tours have no page. The fourth,
  "Official site", cannot get a page under that name; leave it with Not now.
- **Searches no cluster takes (groups right 7/10, suggested cluster right 4/8).** The groups hold together, e.g.
  dress-code searches in Spanish, Dutch and French; Polish and German tour searches. The nearest-cluster suggestion is
  weak across languages: Finnish ticket searches point to Discount, and backpack questions to Tickets (both should
  go elsewhere). "vatikan karte" (map) groups with "omnia card" (a pass). Treat the suggestion as a hint.
